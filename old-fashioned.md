# Old Fashioned

## Ingredients

- 60ml bourbon
- 7.5ml sugar syrup
- 2 dashes angostura bitters

## Method

Combine all ingredients in a rocks glass. Add a large ice cube and stir for 20–30 seconds until well chilled and diluted. Express an orange twist over the glass and use as garnish.

## Notes

Rye whiskey is a classic alternative to bourbon. A sugar cube muddled with bitters is the traditional method — 7.5ml syrup is the modern equivalent. Do not garnish with a cherry and orange slice — that is a Wisconsin Old Fashioned.
