# Boulevardier

## Ingredients

- 45ml bourbon
- 30ml campari
- 30ml sweet vermouth

## Method

Stir all ingredients with ice for 30 seconds. Strain into a rocks glass over a large ice cube. Garnish with an orange twist or Luxardo cherry.

## Notes

Rye whiskey can be substituted for bourbon for a drier, spicier result. Increase bourbon to 50ml if you prefer a spirit-forward ratio.
