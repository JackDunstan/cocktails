# Mai Tai

## Ingredients

- 30ml aged rum
- 30ml dark rum
- 15ml cointreau
- 15ml orgeat syrup
- 30ml lime juice

## Method

Shake all ingredients with ice. Strain into a rocks glass over crushed ice. Garnish with the spent lime shell, a mint sprig and a cherry.

## Notes

Trader Vic's original recipe calls for 17-year J. Wray & Nephew — use the best aged rum you have. A split of Jamaican and agricole rums is the classic approach. Do not add fruit juice — that is not a Mai Tai.
