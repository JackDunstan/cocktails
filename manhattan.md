# Manhattan

## Ingredients

- 50ml rye whiskey
- 25ml sweet vermouth
- 2 dashes angostura bitters

## Method

Stir all ingredients with ice for 30 seconds. Strain into a chilled coupe. Garnish with a Luxardo cherry.

## Notes

Rye is traditional; bourbon makes a sweeter Manhattan. A dash of orange bitters alongside Angostura adds complexity. Use quality sweet vermouth — Cocchi di Torino is excellent here.
