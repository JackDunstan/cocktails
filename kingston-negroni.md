# Kingston Negroni

## Ingredients

- 30ml aged rum
- 30ml campari
- 30ml sweet vermouth

## Method

Stir all ingredients with ice for 30 seconds. Strain into a rocks glass over a large ice cube. Express an orange twist over the glass and use as garnish.

## Notes

Substitute gin for aged rum — a Jamaican rum works particularly well. Appleton Estate or Hamilton Jamaican Pot Still works beautifully here.
