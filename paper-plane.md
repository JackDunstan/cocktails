# Paper Plane

## Ingredients

- 22.5ml bourbon
- 22.5ml aperol
- 22.5ml amaro nonino
- 22.5ml lemon juice

## Method

Shake all ingredients with ice. Fine strain into a chilled coupe.

## Notes

Equal parts cocktail. Amaro Nonino is not substitutable — it is the defining ingredient. If unavailable, this is a different drink.
