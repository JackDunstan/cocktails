# Corpse Reviver #2

## Ingredients

- 22.5ml gin
- 22.5ml cointreau
- 22.5ml lillet blanc
- 22.5ml lemon juice
- 2 dashes absinthe

## Method

Add a dash or two of absinthe to a chilled coupe, swirl to coat and discard. Shake remaining ingredients with ice. Fine strain into the absinthe-rinsed coupe.

## Notes

Another equal parts classic. Cocchi Americano is a good substitute for Lillet Blanc. The Savoy Cocktail Book warns: 'Four of these taken in swift succession will unrevive the corpse again.'
