# Mezcal Negroni

## Ingredients

- 30ml mezcal
- 30ml campari
- 30ml sweet vermouth

## Method

Stir all ingredients with ice for 30 seconds. Strain into a rocks glass over a large ice cube. Express an orange twist over the glass and use as garnish.

## Notes

Use an espadin mezcal — the smoke should complement, not overwhelm. A 50/50 split of mezcal and gin is a good middle ground if the smokiness is too forward.
