# Margarita

## Ingredients

- 50ml tequila blanco
- 25ml cointreau
- 25ml lime juice
- 5ml agave syrup

## Method

Shake all ingredients with ice. Fine strain into a chilled, salt-rimmed coupe.

## Notes

Cointreau can be substituted with triple sec. For a Tommy's Margarita, replace Cointreau with agave syrup (15ml).
