# French 75

## Ingredients

- 30ml gin
- 15ml lemon juice
- 10ml sugar syrup
- 60ml champagne

## Method

Shake gin, lemon juice and sugar syrup with ice. Fine strain into a flute or coupe. Top with chilled Champagne. Garnish with a lemon twist.

## Notes

Cognac is the New Orleans variation and equally valid. Use a decent but not precious Champagne — or a good Crémant. Prosecco will work but is sweeter.
