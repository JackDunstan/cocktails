# Mojito

## Ingredients

- 50ml white rum
- 25ml lime juice
- 15ml sugar syrup
- 8 leaves fresh mint
- 75ml soda water

## Method

Gently press mint leaves in the base of a highball glass — do not muddle aggressively, you want to bruise not shred. Add rum, lime juice and sugar syrup. Fill glass with crushed ice. Add soda water and stir gently to combine. Top with more crushed ice and garnish with a mint sprig and lime wedge.

## Notes

Light rum is traditional — Havana Club 3 or Bacardi. Do not muddle the mint aggressively or you will release bitter chlorophyll. The mint should be fragrant, not green-stained.
