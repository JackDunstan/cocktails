# Jungle Bird

## Ingredients

- 45ml dark rum
- 22.5ml campari
- 45ml pineapple juice
- 15ml lime juice
- 7.5ml sugar syrup

## Method

Shake all ingredients with ice. Strain into a rocks glass over crushed ice. Garnish with a pineapple wedge.

## Notes

A Kuala Lumpur classic from the Aviary Bar, 1978. The bitterness of Campari against pineapple is the defining characteristic — do not reduce it.
