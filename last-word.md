# Last Word

## Ingredients

- 22.5ml gin
- 22.5ml green chartreuse
- 22.5ml maraschino liqueur
- 22.5ml lime juice

## Method

Shake all ingredients with ice. Fine strain into a chilled coupe.

## Notes

Another equal parts classic. Luxardo is the benchmark maraschino liqueur. Yellow Chartreuse can substitute for a gentler herbal note.
