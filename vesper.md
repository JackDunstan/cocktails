# Vesper

## Ingredients

- 45ml gin
- 15ml vodka
- 7.5ml lillet blanc

## Method

Shake all ingredients hard with ice. Fine strain into a chilled coupe. Garnish with a large lemon twist.

## Notes

Shaken, not stirred — as specified by Fleming. Lillet Blanc can be substituted with Cocchi Americano for a more bitter, closer-to-original profile. Original recipe used Kina Lillet, no longer available.
