# Sazerac

## Ingredients

- 60ml rye whiskey
- 7.5ml sugar syrup
- 3 dashes peychaud's bitters
- 5ml absinthe

## Method

Rinse a chilled rocks glass with absinthe, discarding the excess. In a mixing glass, stir rye, sugar syrup and bitters with ice for 30 seconds. Strain into the absinthe-rinsed glass. Express a lemon twist over the drink, rub around the rim, then discard. No ice.

## Notes

Served without ice — this is correct. Peychaud's bitters are non-negotiable here. Herbsaint or Pernod can substitute for absinthe in the rinse.
