# Black Manhattan

## Ingredients

- 50ml rye whiskey
- 25ml averna amaro
- 1 dash angostura bitters
- 1 dash orange bitters

## Method

Stir all ingredients with ice for 30 seconds. Strain into a chilled coupe. Garnish with a Luxardo cherry.

## Notes

Averna replaces sweet vermouth for a richer, more bitter profile. Created by Todd Smith at Bourbon & Branch, San Francisco.
