# Sidecar

## Ingredients

- 50ml cognac
- 25ml cointreau
- 20ml lemon juice

## Method

Shake all ingredients with ice. Fine strain into a chilled, sugar-rimmed coupe. Garnish with a lemon twist.

## Notes

VSOP cognac or better. Cointreau is preferred over generic triple sec here — the quality matters in a three-ingredient drink.
