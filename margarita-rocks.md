# Margarita on the Rocks

## Ingredients

- 50ml tequila blanco
- 25ml cointreau
- 25ml lime juice
- 5ml agave syrup

## Method

Shake all ingredients with ice. Strain into a salt-rimmed rocks glass over a large ice cube.

## Notes

Same recipe as the straight-up version — serve over ice for a longer, more casual drink.
