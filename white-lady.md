# White Lady

## Ingredients

- 40ml gin
- 20ml cointreau
- 20ml lemon juice
- 15ml egg white

## Method

Dry shake all ingredients without ice first to emulsify the egg white. Add ice and shake hard. Fine strain into a chilled coupe.

## Notes

The dry shake is essential for a proper foam. Aquafaba (chickpea water) is a vegan substitute for egg white at the same quantity.
