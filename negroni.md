# Negroni

## Ingredients

- 30ml gin
- 30ml campari
- 30ml sweet vermouth

## Method

Stir all ingredients with ice for 30 seconds. Strain into a rocks glass over a large ice cube. Express an orange twist over the glass and use as garnish.

## Notes

Use a quality sweet vermouth such as Carpano Antica or Cocchi di Torino. Refresh vermouth bottles regularly — refrigerate after opening.
